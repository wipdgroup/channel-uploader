Channel Uploader

A private publishing tool for its owner's YouTube channels

Channel Uploader is a small script that its owner runs on their own computer to publish videos to YouTube channels they own or manage. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload those channels' videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedule.

It is not offered to the public. The only people who sign in to it are the owner and the managers of the owner's channels.

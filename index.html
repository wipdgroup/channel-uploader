Candlelit Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Candlelit Sleep Stories YouTube channel

Candlelit Uploader is a small script that the owner of the Candlelit Sleep Stories YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedule.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

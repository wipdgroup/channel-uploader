Privacy Policy

Candlelit Uploader · Effective October 24, 2026

What this app is

Candlelit Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Candlelit Sleep Stories YouTube channel to manage that channel's own videos. It uses the YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app accesses the owner's YouTube account to:

  • upload the channel's videos;
  • set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and scheduled publish times;
  • read the upload and processing status of the channel's videos.

It does not access any other Google account or any other user's data.

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics, and does not store, sell or share YouTube data with anyone.

YouTube and Google

This app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Deleting the token file from the computer it runs on also ends the app's access.

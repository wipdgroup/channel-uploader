Privacy Policy

Channel Uploader · Effective October 24, 2026

What this app is

Channel Uploader is a private tool its owner uses to manage the videos of YouTube channels they own or manage. It uses the YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

When a channel's owner or manager grants permission through Google's sign-in screen, the app accesses that YouTube channel to:

  • upload the channel's videos;
  • set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, playlists and scheduled publish times;
  • read the upload and processing status of the channel's videos.

It only accesses channels whose owner or manager has signed in to it, and nothing else in their Google Account.

How data is stored

Each sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the app owner's own computer. The app has no server, collects no analytics, and does not store, sell or share YouTube data with anyone.

YouTube and Google

This app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party access settings. Deleting a channel's token file from the computer the app runs on also ends its access to that channel.
